When a conversation is about to outgrow what cx can hold, cx asks Jev which results of earlier steps are no longer needed and clears them, so the summary comes later. Every word you and cx wrote stays.

Data you paid for is cleared only once it is saved in your files. Jev reads what you and cx wrote and what each step did, but not what a step's result says.

## What it costs

Jev is paid from your balance or with your own TypeSafe API key.

## What it needs

It needs Jev, which Plugins installs with it.
